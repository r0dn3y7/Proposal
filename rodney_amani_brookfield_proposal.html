About Me

The person behind the programme.

My name is Rodney Amani. I am a 21-year-old Moringa School graduate with a strong interest in software development and technology. I want to use the skills I have developed to introduce young people to coding in a practical, engaging and approachable way.

My approach

Students should not only learn what code means — they should use it to make something. My proposed lessons would combine short explanations, guided practice, challenges and projects so students can see their progress and build confidence as they learn.

The Proposal

Bring coding in as a Brookfield Academy extracurricular activity.

Brookfield Academy does not currently have this programme. I am proposing that the school consider introducing a structured coding and digital skills club/activity for interested students.

🎯

What I am proposing

A practical programme where students progressively learn programming and web development through projects.

  • ✓ Weekly extracurricular sessions
  • ✓ Beginner-friendly lessons
  • ✓ Hands-on coding exercises
  • ✓ Individual and group projects
  • ✓ Student project presentations
💡

Why it can benefit students

Coding provides a practical way to develop digital confidence alongside creativity and structured problem-solving.

  • ✓ Logical and analytical thinking
  • ✓ Creativity and independent learning
  • ✓ Digital literacy
  • ✓ Collaboration and communication
  • ✓ Early exposure to technology careers
